Frequently asked questions

Can I pre-order before I arrive?

Not yet. Place your order at the counter and you will get a token number. Most orders are ready in about 10 minutes.

Is the food halal?

Yes. All meat served at Campus Bites is halal.

What if my order is wrong?

Tell the staff at the counter with your token and we will fix it. You can also send a message on the feedback page.

Do you open during exams and holidays?

Hours can change during exam weeks and university holidays. Any change is posted at the counter.

Got a suggestion or a complaint?

We read every message and use your feedback to improve the menu.

How to Order

Ordering at Campus Bites takes four simple steps.

  1. Choose your items. Check the menu or today's deals.
  2. Pay at the counter. We accept cash and student card payments.
  3. Collect your token. Keep the token number safe.
  4. Pick up your order. Wait for your number to be called.

Peak hours

12:30 PM – 2:00 PM is our busiest time. Order before 12:30 PM to skip the queue.

Pick-up rules

  • Orders must be collected within 15 minutes.
  • Show your token at the pick-up window.
  • Return trays to the rack after eating.
